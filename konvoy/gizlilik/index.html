Hatırlatma bildirimleri

Oyun bir kez "Hatırlatayım mı?" diye sorar; EVET dersen telefonun bildirim izni ister. Hatırlatmalar (günlük ödül, bedava çark, sandıklar, günlük seri, yeni yol) telefonunun kendisinde, akşam saatine kurulur; günde en fazla bir tane gelir. Hiçbir sunucu kullanılmaz, bildirim için bir veri gönderilmez. Hatırlatmaları oyunda Ayarlar'dan ya da telefonunun bildirim ayarlarından kapatabilirsin.

Uygulama içi satın almalar

Oyun App Store'da hiçbir şey satmaz ve satın alma kodu içermez; altın, yakıt, sandıklar ve şans çarkı yalnızca oynayarak ya da isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklam izleyerek kazanılır. Oyundaki MAĞAZA'da yakıt yalnızca oyunda kazanılan altınla ya da ödüllü videolarla (4 video = 1 yakıt) alınır.

Android sürümünde isteğe bağlı iki satın alma vardır: Reklamsız (bir kez alınır, tur aralarındaki reklamları kaldırır) ve 3'lü yakıt. Oyunun tamamı hiçbir şey satın almadan oynanır.

  • Ödemeyi Google Play alır. Kart ve ödeme bilgilerini mağazaya girersin; Turbo Games bunları görmez ve saklamaz.
  • Oyun satın alma için Google Play Faturalandırma'yı kullanır: ürünleri ve fiyatlarını getirmek, satın almayı başlatmak ve önceki satın almaları geri yüklemek için cihazın Google Play ile konuşur. Google bu bilgileri kendi politikasına göre işler: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Oyun yalnızca sonucu (reklamsız açık mı, depodaki yakıt) ve aynı satın almanın iki kez verilmemesi için mağazanın verdiği işlem numaralarını cihazında saklar; bunlar hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez. Satın almada kullanılan Unity IAP bileşeni bu oyunda Unity'ye veri göndermeyecek şekilde kuruludur (Unity Gaming Services ve satın alma analizi kullanılmaz).
  • Reklamsız, aynı mağaza hesabıyla başka bir cihazda ya da oyunu yeniden kurunca MAĞAZA → SATIN ALIMLARI GERİ YÜKLE ile geri gelir. Yakıt cihazda saklandığı için oyunu silersen harcanmamış yakıt geri gelmez.
  • Satın alma geçmişini ve iade taleplerini Google Play hesabından yönetirsin.

Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyunda ödüllü ("reklam izle, ödül kazan") ve geçiş reklamları vardır; bunları Google AdMob gösterir. Ödüllü reklam yalnız REKLAM İZLE düğmesine dokununca açılır; geçiş reklamı ilk 5 turda hiç çıkmaz, sonra en fazla 3 tur başlangıcında bir ve en az 45 saniye arayla çıkar. Reklam gösterebilmek ve ölçebilmek için oyundaki Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı şu verileri toplayıp Google'a gönderir:

  • cihaz tanımlayıcıları (iPhone ve iPad'de uygulamaya / geliştiriciye bağlı kimlik ve izin verdiysen reklam kimliği IDFA, Android'de reklam kimliği),
  • IP adresinden tahmin edilen yaklaşık konum,
  • gösterilen reklamlar ve reklamlarla etkileşim,
  • performans, çökme ve tanılama verileri.

Bu veriler reklam gösterimi, reklam ölçümü, sahtekârlığın önlenmesi ve analiz için kullanılır; Google bunları kendi politikasına göre işler: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Turbo Games bu verileri kendisi toplamaz, saklamaz; AdMob'dan yalnızca kişiye ait olmayan toplam reklam raporlarını görür.

  • Oyunda Ayarlar → HAKKINDA → REKLAMLAR HAKKINDA ekranı, oyundan çıkmadan, bir reklam seçilirken kullanılabilen bilgileri ve (iPhone ve iPad'de) bu cihazdaki izleme izninin durumunu gösterir.
  • iPhone ve iPad'de Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı, reklamlardan gelen uygulama yüklemelerini ölçmek için Apple'ın SKAdNetwork'ünü ve bir reklama dokununca App Store sayfasını oyunun içinde açmak için StoreKit'i kullanır. Bunlar bir şey satmaz.

  • Android'de reklamsız satın aldıysan tur aralarındaki geçiş reklamları gösterilmez. İsteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar yine izlenebilir; Google'ın SDK'sı onlar için yukarıdaki verileri işlemeye devam eder.

İzin verirsen Google, reklamları sana göre seçmek ve ölçmek için cihazının reklam kimliğini (IDFA) kullanabilir; bu, diğer şirketlerin uygulama ve sitelerinden toplanan verilerle birleştirilebilir. İzin vermezsen reklam kimliği kullanılmaz, reklamlar yine gösterilir ve oyun aynen çalışır. English

Turbo Games has no server of its own and, except for emails you send us, does not collect, store or sell personal data about you. The data that Google AdMob, which shows the ads in the game, collects through the game and how it is used are described in the "Ads" section below. The third parties that can access your data through the game (Google for ads; Apple or Google for leaderboards) are services that provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy.

  • Your progress, gold, fuel, garage choices, missions, achievements, records, settings and (on Android) the outcome of your purchases (whether Ad-Free is on) are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game; on Android, Ad-Free can be restored with the same store account (see "In-app purchases" below).
  • The game does not access location, contacts, photos, the microphone or similar permissions and data. It uses vibration. Notification permission is requested only for optional reminders, and only if you agree (see "Reminder notifications" below).
  • The game has no server, account or sign-up form of its own. The whole game can be played without the internet.

Google Play Games

If Google Play Games is on your phone, the game may sign you in to Google Play Games automatically when it starts. This is optional: the whole game can be played without signing in. While signed in:

  • Your endless mode scores are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards and the achievements you unlock are sent to Google Play Games.
  • This service is provided by Google. Google processes your Play Games player identity (gamer name, avatar), scores, achievements, and analytics and diagnostics data for the stability of the service under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Turbo Games does not collect or store this data itself; it can only see what other players can see on the leaderboards.
  • You can manage or delete your Play Games profile, scores and achievements in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com). You can also turn off automatic sign-in to games in the Play Games settings.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, leaderboards and achievements are run by Apple's Game Center. This is optional too: the whole game can be played without signing in to Game Center. While signed in, your endless mode scores are sent to Game Center leaderboards and the achievements you unlock are sent to Game Center. Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); Turbo Games does not collect or store it. You can manage or turn off Game Center in Settings → Game Center on your iPhone.

The SHARE button on the results screen opens your phone's share menu; you choose what to share and with whom, and the game does not send anything by itself.

Reminder notifications

The game asks once, "Want a reminder?"; if you say YES, your phone asks for notification permission. Reminders (daily reward, free spin, crates, daily streak, new road) are scheduled on your phone itself for the evening, at most one a day. No server is used and no data is sent for notifications. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Emails you send us

If you email us for support or with ABOUT → REPORT AN INAPPROPRIATE AD, we receive your email address and what you write. For ad reports your email app opens with the game version, device model and system version filled in; you can delete them before sending. We use this only to answer you and to pass the reported ad on to Google; we do not share or sell it. Emails are deleted at the latest 12 months after the matter is closed; if you want them deleted sooner, just write to us.

In-app purchases

The iPhone and iPad version has no in-app purchases. The game sells nothing on the App Store and contains no purchase code; gold, fuel, crates and the lucky wheel are earned only by playing or by watching optional rewarded ads. In the in-game SHOP, fuel is only available for gold earned in the game or for rewarded videos (4 videos = 1 fuel).

The Android version has two optional purchases: Ad-Free (bought once, removes the ads between runs) and a 3-fuel pack. The whole game can be played without buying anything.

  • Payment is taken by Google Play. You enter your card and payment details in the store; Turbo Games never sees or stores them.
  • For purchases the game uses Google Play Billing: your device talks to Google Play to load products and prices, start a purchase and restore earlier purchases. Google processes this under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • The game only stores the outcome (whether Ad-Free is on, the fuel in your tank) and the transaction numbers the store provides, so the same purchase isn't delivered twice, on your device; none of this is sent to any server. The Unity IAP component used for purchases is set up in this game to send no data to Unity (Unity Gaming Services and purchase analytics are not used).
  • Ad-Free comes back on another device or after reinstalling, with the same store account, via SHOP → RESTORE PURCHASES. Fuel is stored on the device, so unused fuel is lost if you uninstall the game.
  • You manage your purchase history and refund requests in your Google Play account.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ("watch an ad, get a reward") and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. A rewarded ad only starts when you tap a WATCH AD button; interstitials never appear in your first 5 runs, then at most once every 3 run starts and at least 45 seconds apart. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects the following data and sends it to Google:

  • device identifiers (an app- / developer-scoped identifier on iPhone and iPad and, if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier IDFA; the advertising ID on Android),
  • approximate location estimated from the IP address,
  • the ads shown and your interactions with them,
  • performance, crash and diagnostics data.

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and for analytics; Google processes it under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Turbo Games does not collect or store this data itself; it only sees aggregated, non-personal ad reports in AdMob.

  • In the game, Settings → ABOUT → ABOUT ADS shows, without leaving the game, the information that may be used to choose an ad and (on iPhone and iPad) the tracking permission status on this device.
  • On iPhone and iPad the Google Mobile Ads SDK uses Apple's SKAdNetwork to measure app installs from ads and StoreKit to open an App Store page inside the game when you tap an ad. Neither sells anything.
  • Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) stores your choice on your phone to remember it; your choice and the device information needed to show the form go to Google.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • If you bought Ad-Free on Android, no interstitial ads are shown between runs. Optional rewarded ads can still be watched, and Google's SDK keeps processing the data above for them.
  • On iPhone and iPad the game shows Apple's tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency) once, before ads start. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to select and measure ads for you, which may be combined with data collected from other companies' apps and websites. If you don't, the advertising identifier is not used, ads are still shown and the game works the same. In Europe, if you did not consent to personalized ads or to Google in Google's consent form, the tracking prompt is not shown at all. You can change your choice at any time in your phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form at launch; you can change your choice later in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • If you see an inappropriate ad, tap Settings → ABOUT → REPORT AN INAPPROPRIATE AD in the game (your email app opens to ynsemr0506@gmail.com with a few questions filled in) or write to that address directly; we report the ad to Google and ask for it to be removed from the ad network.
  • In Türkiye (on Android) the game asks for your consent to personalized ads on the first menu.
