English

Konvoy does not collect or share any personal data.

  • The game does not use an internet connection. There are no accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • Your progress, gold, garage choices and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • The game does not access location, contacts, photos, the microphone or any other personal data. The only permission it uses is vibration.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

If ads are added to the game in the future, this policy will be updated to explain which data the ad provider (for example Google AdMob) collects, such as the device's advertising ID.
