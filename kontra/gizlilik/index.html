  • Kredilerin, arabaların, modifiyelerin, boya ve kaplamaların, plakaların, kariyer yıldızların, rekorların, hayalet turların, günlük ödüllerin ve ayarların önce cihazında saklanır. İnternet varken bu ilerlemenin bir yedeği Unity'nin bulutuna da gönderilir (aşağıda "Hesap, bulut yedeği, sıralamalar ve kulüpler").

Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyunda ödüllü ("reklam izle, ödül kazan"; hep isteğe bağlı) ve yarışlar arasında geçiş reklamları vardır; bunları Google AdMob gösterir. Yarış sırasında reklam gösterilmez. REKLAMSIZ paketini alırsan geçiş reklamları kapanır. Reklam gösterebilmek ve ölçebilmek için oyundaki Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı şu verileri toplayıp Google'a gönderir:

  • cihaz tanımlayıcıları (Android'de reklam kimliği; iPhone ve iPad'de uygulamaya / geliştiriciye bağlı kimlik ve izin verdiysen reklam kimliği IDFA),
  • IP adresinden tahmin edilen yaklaşık konum,
  • gösterilen reklamlar ve reklamlarla etkileşim,
  • performans, çökme ve tanılama verileri.

Bu veriler reklam gösterimi, reklam ölçümü, sahtekârlığın önlenmesi ve analiz için kullanılır; Google bunları kendi politikasına göre işler: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Turbo Games bu verileri kendisi toplamaz, saklamaz; AdMob'dan yalnızca kişiye ait olmayan toplam reklam raporlarını görür.

  • Reklamlar her yaşa uygun içerikle sınırlıdır.
  • Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, İngiltere ve İsviçre'de oyun reklamlar başlamadan önce Google'ın izin ekranını gösterir; tercihini sonradan AYARLAR → GİZLİLİK'ten değiştirebilirsin.
  • Türkiye'de oyun (Android'de) ilk garajda kişiselleştirilmiş reklam için iznini sorar. İzin verirsen Google reklamları ilgi alanlarına göre seçebilir; izin vermezsen ya da henüz cevap vermediysen reklamlar kişiselleştirilmeden istenir. Tercihini istediğin zaman AYARLAR → GİZLİLİK'ten değiştirebilirsin.
  • iPhone ve iPad'de oyun, reklamlar başlamadan önce Apple'ın izleme izni penceresini (App Tracking Transparency) bir kez gösterir. İzin verirsen Google, reklamları sana göre seçmek ve ölçmek için cihazının reklam kimliğini (IDFA) kullanabilir; izin vermezsen reklam kimliği kullanılmaz, reklamlar yine gösterilir ve oyun aynen çalışır. Kararını Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → İzleme bölümünden değiştirebilirsin.
  • Android'de reklam kimliğini telefonunun Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar bölümünden sıfırlayabilir ya da silebilirsin.
  • İnternet yoksa reklam gösterilmez; oyun yine tam olarak oynanır.

Hesap, bulut yedeği, sıralamalar ve kulüpler (Unity Gaming Services)

Oyun açılırken Unity Gaming Services (UGS) cihazında anonim bir oyuncu kimliği oluşturur; adın ya da e-postan istenmez. Bu kimlikle:

  • Bulut yedeği: ilerlemen (kredi, seviye, arabalar, modifiyeler, ödüller, kariyer, Drift Pass) Unity Cloud Save'e yedeklenir. Cihaza özgü ayarlar (ses, grafik, dil) gönderilmez.
  • Hesap (isteğe bağlı): AYARLAR → HESAP'tan kendi seçtiğin bir kullanıcı adı ve şifreyle hesap açarsan yedeğini başka telefonda geri alabilirsin. Şifren Unity'nin kimlik doğrulama hizmetinde saklanır; Turbo Games şifreni görmez. E-posta, telefon numarası ya da gerçek ad istenmez.
  • Sıralamalar: yarış bittiğinde puanın, sürücü adın, ülke kodun, arabanın adı, kulüp etiketin ve sürücü seviyen haftalık drift, harita rekoru ve dereceli lig tablolarına gönderilir; bu tablolar diğer oyunculara gösterilir.
  • Kulüpler: bir kulüp kurar ya da kulübe katılırsan kulüp adı, etiketi, üyelerin sürücü adları ve kulübe kattıkları puanlar kulübün diğer üyelerine ve kulüp sıralamasında herkese gösterilir.

Bu veriler bizim adımıza Unity Technologies tarafından işlenir (veri işleyen): https://unity.com/legal/developer-privacy-policy. Hesabını ve buluttaki verini istediğin zaman silebilirsin (aşağıda "Hesabını silme").

Çok oyunculu mod (Photon)

English

Turbo Games does not run its own servers and does not sell personal data about you. The game uses the service providers below; each section says what data it processes and why: Google AdMob for ads, Photon for multiplayer, Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) for voice chat, accounts, cloud backup, leaderboards, clubs, analytics and crash reports, and Google Play / the App Store for purchases.

  • Your credits, cars, modifications, paint and liveries, license plates, career stars, records, ghost laps, daily rewards and settings are stored on your device first. When you are online, a backup of this progress is also sent to Unity's cloud (see "Account, cloud backup, leaderboards and clubs").
  • Creating an account is optional. Single-player is fully playable offline; the internet is used for ads, cloud backup, leaderboards, clubs and the optional multiplayer mode (including voice chat).
  • The game does not access your precise location, contacts, camera or files. For the country leaderboard the game reads the country code from your phone's SIM / network or language settings (e.g. "TR"); GPS is not used. The microphone is used only when you want to talk in voice chat in a multiplayer room, after asking for your permission.
  • When you tap SHOOT in photo mode, the in-game photo you took is saved to your phone's gallery on Android and to the game's own folder on iPhone. The game does not read other photos in your gallery and never sends any photo anywhere.
  • A video you make with CLIP on the replay screen is saved to the game's folder on your device; if you tap SAVE TO GALLERY it goes to your gallery, and if you tap SHARE or TIKTOK it goes to the app you choose, at your request. The game never sends videos anywhere by itself; the app you share to handles the video under its own policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads ("watch an ad, get a reward"; always optional) and interstitial ads between races; Google AdMob shows them. No ads are shown during a race. Buying NO ADS turns interstitial ads off. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects the following data and sends it to Google:

  • device identifiers (on Android the advertising ID; on iPhone and iPad an app/developer-scoped identifier and, if you allow it, the advertising identifier IDFA),
  • approximate location estimated from the IP address,
  • the ads shown and your interactions with them,
  • performance, crash and diagnostic data.

This data is used for showing ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention and analytics; Google processes it under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Turbo Games does not collect or store this data itself; from AdMob it only sees aggregate ad reports that do not identify you.

  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent screen before ads start; you can change your choice later in SETTINGS → PRIVACY.
  • In Türkiye the game (on Android) asks for your consent to personalized ads in the first garage. If you allow it, Google may choose ads based on your interests; if you don't, or haven't answered yet, ads are requested without personalization. You can change your choice at any time in SETTINGS → PRIVACY.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game shows Apple's tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency) once before ads start. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to choose and measure ads for you; if you don't, the advertising identifier is not used, ads are still shown and the game works the same. You can change your decision in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Without an internet connection no ads are shown; the game is still fully playable.

Account, cloud backup, leaderboards and clubs (Unity Gaming Services)

When the game starts, Unity Gaming Services (UGS) creates an anonymous player ID on your device; no name or email is asked for. With this ID:

  • Cloud backup: your progress (credits, level, cars, modifications, rewards, career, Drift Pass) is backed up to Unity Cloud Save. Device-specific settings (sound, graphics, language) are not sent.
  • Account (optional): if you create an account in SETTINGS → ACCOUNT with a username and password of your choice, you can restore your backup on another phone. Your password is stored by Unity's authentication service; Turbo Games never sees it. No email, phone number or real name is asked for.
  • Leaderboards: when a race ends, your score, driver name, country code, car name, club tag and driver level are sent to the weekly drift, map record and ranked league tables; these tables are shown to other players.
  • Clubs: if you create or join a club, the club name and tag, the members' driver names and the points they add are shown to the club's other members and, in the club leaderboard, to everyone.

Unity Technologies processes this data on our behalf (as processor): https://unity.com/legal/developer-privacy-policy. You can delete your account and cloud data at any time (see "Deleting your account").

Analytics and crash reports (Unity)

To improve the game, it sends Unity Analytics usage events that do not identify you by name (such as session started, race finished, map, mode, car, score, reward, Drift Pass tier), the game version, device model, country code and the anonymous player ID; if the game hits an error or crashes, it sends the error message and technical stack information to Unity Cloud Diagnostics. In Türkiye, if you answer "no" to the game's KVKK question, these analytics events are not sent. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold; Unity processes it on our behalf.

Purchases

The game may offer optional real-money purchases (credit packs, NO ADS, some cars, Drift Pass premium). Payment is taken and processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone); Turbo Games never sees your card details. The game only receives the store's purchase confirmation and product ID and uses them to deliver the item. Purchases are subject to Google's and Apple's own policies. There are no paid random-content boxes (loot boxes).

Multiplayer (Photon)

If you enter MULTIPLAYER, the game connects to the game servers of Exit Games (Photon, Germany / USA; Europe region). So that you can drive and chat with the other players in a room, the following is relayed through these servers to the players in your room:

  • the driver name you choose, your car model and paint, the room settings (map, time, weather) and a hash of the optional room password,
  • your car's position, speed and state while driving, and your score in a drift battle,
  • the messages you write in room chat (they pass through a profanity filter and are shown to the players in the room),
  • your IP address for the connection (used by Photon's server to establish and maintain it).

Turbo Games does not store this data; Photon processes it on our behalf only to run the game: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy. When you tap REPORT, the player's name and messages are, for now, saved only on your device; to report a player to us, write to the email address below. If you never enter MULTIPLAYER, this connection is never made.

Voice chat (Vivox, Unity)

When voice chat is on in a multiplayer room, the game connects to Unity Technologies' Vivox voice service with the anonymous player ID above. You are asked for microphone permission the first time you want to talk; if you decline, you can only listen.

  • Your voice is transmitted in real time to the players in your room only while you hold the push-to-talk button, or while OPEN MIC is selected and you have not muted yourself. Turbo Games does not record or store voice.
